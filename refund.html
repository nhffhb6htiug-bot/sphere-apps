Cancellation, Refund & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains cancellations, refunds and delivery for jobs booked on Sphere, operated by Sangeeta Devi under the name Sphere by Syahi Films.

In short: your money is held safely after payment. The Editor gets paid only when you, the Client, approve the final video. If there is a problem, either side can report it and Syahi Films decides fairly.

1. How payment protection works

When a Client pays for a job, the Editor’s share is held (escrow). It is released to the Editor only after the Client approves the delivered video, or after Syahi Films resolves a dispute in the Editor’s favour.

2. Full refund cases

  • Cancellation before work starts: the Client cancels within 24 hours of payment and the Editor has not delivered anything — 100% refund.
  • No delivery: the Editor does not deliver within 3 days after the agreed deadline — 100% refund.

3. After the video is delivered

  • There is no automatic refund once the Editor has delivered.
  • The Client should first ask for corrections in the job chat.
  • If the issue is not solved, the Client or the Editor can use Report problem in the app or call Support. Syahi Films reviews the chat, the brief, the raw files and the delivery, and decides one of: release payment to the Editor, a re-edit, a partial refund, or a full refund.

4. Protection for Editors

If a Client does not approve or respond within 7 days of delivery without a valid reason, the Editor can report it. After review, Syahi Films may release the payment to the Editor.

5. Refund timeline

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a few extra days to show the credit.

6. Platform fee

Sphere’s platform fee is deducted from the Editor’s payout. When a full refund is approved, the Client receives the full amount paid.

7. Delivery policy

Sphere provides digital services only; nothing is shipped physically. Editors deliver the final video as a link (for example Google Drive or YouTube) inside the job by the agreed deadline. The job page shows the delivery status at every step.
