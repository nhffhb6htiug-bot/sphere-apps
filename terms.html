1. What Sphere does

Sphere is an online marketplace that connects clients who need video editing (“Clients”) with independent video editors (“Editors”). Sphere provides job posting, bidding, chat, secure payments and dispute support. Editors are independent professionals, not employees of Sphere.

2. Eligibility and accounts

3. Editor verification

Editors submit samples that Syahi Films reviews before granting a “Verified” badge and code. Verification is a quality check of submitted samples only; it is not a guarantee of any future result.

4. Jobs, pricing and platform fee

  • Clients post jobs or hire an Editor directly. The final price is agreed and locked inside the app before payment.
  • Clients pay only the locked amount. Sphere keeps a 5% platform fee, which is deducted from the Editor’s payout. Editors see their exact take-home amount before accepting.

5. Payments and escrow

  • Payments are processed by Razorpay. Sphere does not store card, UPI or bank login details.
  • After payment, the Editor’s share is held and is released only when the Client approves the final delivery, or when Syahi Films decides a dispute in the Editor’s favour.
  • Editor payouts are sent through Razorpay to the Editor’s linked bank account, or manually by Syahi Films where a linked account is not yet set up. Bank settlement timelines apply.
  • Arranging payment outside Sphere for work found on Sphere is not allowed and removes our payment protection and dispute support.

6. Delivery of services

All services are digital. Raw footage and final videos are shared as links (for example Google Drive or YouTube) inside the job. No physical goods are shipped. Delivery timelines are the deadline agreed in the job.
