Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sphere by Syahi Films (“Sphere”, “we”), owned and operated by Sangeeta Devi, collects and uses your information when you use https://sphere-live.onrender.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, mobile number and your role (Client or Editor).
  • Google Sign-In: if you log in with Google we receive your name and email address. We use them only to create and secure your account.
  • Editor profile: category, skills, experience, price, sample and portfolio links, photos you upload, and verification status.
  • Jobs and communication: job details, bids, chat messages, delivery links, ratings, reviews and reports.
  • Payments: Razorpay processes payments. We receive only the order ID, payment ID and payment status — never your card, UPI PIN or bank login.

2. How we use it

  • To run Sphere: accounts, matching Clients with Editors, chat, notifications and payments.
  • To verify Editors, prevent fraud, and resolve disputes and reports.
  • To contact you about your jobs, payments or support requests.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • Verified Editor profiles (name, photo, category, skills, price, portfolio) are visible to logged-in users.
  • Your contact details are visible only to the other party of a job, bid or chat you are part of, and to Syahi Films admins.
  • Service providers who help us run Sphere: Supabase (database and hosting), Render (website hosting), Razorpay (payments) and Google (sign-in and the Gemini API that powers Sphere AI). They process data only to provide their service.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

4. Google user data

Sphere’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only use your Google name and email for sign-in.

4a. Sphere AI

When you use Sphere AI, your messages and basic context (your name and role, and the public profiles of verified editors) are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a reply. Google may use data sent through its free tier to improve its products. Please do not share passwords, OTPs, bank or card details, or other sensitive personal information with Sphere AI. Chat history with Sphere AI is not stored by Sphere.

5. Security and storage

Data is stored with Supabase. Access rules ensure each user can read only their own data and the data of jobs they are part of. Payments are handled by Razorpay’s PCI-DSS compliant systems. No method is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

6. Retention and your rights

We keep your data while your account is active and as long as needed for payments, disputes and legal requirements. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing niteshkumarmatho37@gmail.com. Some records (such as payment records) may be kept where the law requires.

7. Cookies and local storage

Sphere uses your browser’s storage only to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies.

8. Children

Sphere is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
