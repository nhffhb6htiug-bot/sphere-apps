Delete your Sphere account

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sphere and log in.
  2. Go to Profile and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Can't open the app? Ask us

Message Sphere Support on WhatsApp from the phone number of your account, and write "Delete my Sphere account". We delete it within 7 days and confirm on WhatsApp.

WhatsApp 1 WhatsApp 2

10 AM – 7 PM, Monday – Saturday

What is deleted

  • Your login (phone / email / Google), name, profile photo, bio and portfolio
  • Your chats, bids, saved editors, notifications and AI assistant usage
  • Your projects, reviews and uploaded files

What may be kept

  • Payment records held by our payment partner Razorpay, for as long as Indian tax and payment laws require.
  • Uploaded preview videos are removed automatically 15 days after a project ends.

Before you delete

You can't delete an account while a project is still running or while Sphere still has to send you a payment. Finish or cancel those first, or contact Support.
